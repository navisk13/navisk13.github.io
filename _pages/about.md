👋 Hello! I'm Navaneeth (Navi), a cybersecurity enthusiast with a passion for technology. I currently work as a Jr. Cyber Security Analyst at Adelaide University.

🎓 I hold a master's degree in cybersecurity from The University of Adelaide, complemented by a bachelor's degree in computer science and engineering from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, India.

💻 I have a strong foundation in cybersecurity, with expertise spanning Web Security, Network Security, and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC). I actively hone my vulnerability analysis and penetration testing skills and have a working knowledge of frameworks such as NIST CSF & RMF, ASD Essential 8, and ISO 27001. These experiences have deepened my understanding of cybersecurity from both technical and managerial perspectives. I am also working on expanding my expertise in cyber defence and blue teaming to further strengthen my skills. Additionally, my background in software development enables me to adapt quickly to new technologies - a skill I’ve refined through developing and implementing various research-driven solutions across several technical domains, including LLMs & AI and Infrastructure as Code.

✈️ Outside of work, I indulge in my passion for travel, immerse myself in reading, and enjoy swimming. I also explore my creative side as an amateur photographer.
